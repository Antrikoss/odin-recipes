Air fryer rib-eye steaks with sweet potatoes

Description

If you have an air fryer, then you have to prepare these rib-eye steaks! Juicy and perfectly cooked, they are accompanied with sweet potato wedges for a complete and light meal!

Ingredients

  • 2 beef steaks, rib-eye, 700 g
  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon(s) thyme, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon(s) paprika, sweet
  • 1 teaspoon(s) honey
  • salt
  • pepper
  • 500 g sweet potatoes
  • ½ teaspoon(s) paprika, smoked
  • 1 tablespoon(s) thyme, finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoon(s) olive oil

Steps

  1. Put the olive oil, thyme, paprika, honey, salt, and pepper into a big bowl, and mix them with a spoon.

  2. Transfer the steaks to the bowl and mix with your hands until they are well marinated.

  3. Cut the sweet potatoes into wedges, skin-on, and transfer them to a second bowl.

  4. Add the paprika, thyme, olive oil, salt, and pepper, and mix them with a spoon.

  5. Cook the steaks separately for 15 minutes at 200°C (390°F), and the sweet potatoes for 20 minutes at the same temperature. Halfway through the cooking time, flip the steaks over and toss the sweet potatoes.

  6. Remove the steaks and sweet potatoes from the air fryer’s basket, and serve.
