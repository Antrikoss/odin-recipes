One-pot pasta Bolognese

Description

Whip up one of the easiest recipes for spaghetti Bolognese! Using only one pot, this dish features a rich sauce with ground beef and tomato for a hearty and comforting meal!

Ingredients

  • 500 g spaghetti
  • 700 g ground beef1 (32 ounce) jar spaghetti sauce
  • 1 onion
  • 1 clove(s) of garlic
  • 3-4 sprig(s) thyme
  • 3 tablespoon(s) olive oil
  • 3 tablespoon(s) tomato paste
  • 1 tablespoon(s) granulated sugar
  • 80 g white wine
  • 400 g grated tomatoes
  • 1 chicken bouillon cube
  • 1,5 liter water

Steps

  1. Place a pot over high heat and let it heat up well.

  2. Finely chop the onion, garlic, and thyme.

  3. Add the olive oil, onion, garlic, and thyme to the pot, mix with a wooden spoon, and sauté the onion for 1-2 minutes.

  4. Add the ground beef and sauté it for 10 minutes, stirring with the wooden spoon until nicely browned.

  5. Add the tomato paste and sugar, mix well, and keep sauteing for 1 more minute.

  6. Deglaze the pot with the wine, add the tomatoes, chicken bouillon cube, and water, and stir well.

  7. Add the pasta and boil it for 10-12 minutes, stirring occasionally with a serving spoon.

  8. Remove the pot from the heat, stir the pasta, and divide it among plates.

  9. Serve with grated parmesan and finely chopped parsley.
